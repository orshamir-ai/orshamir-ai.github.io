Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 22, 2026

You can permanently delete your NudgeIt account and all associated data at any time — directly inside the app, or by email if you can't access it. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed. Deletion is free, self-service, and irreversible.

Section 1

Delete Your Account In the App

📱

Android (NudgeIt phone app)

  1. Open the NudgeIt app on your Android phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open the Account Management section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Read and confirm the deletion prompt.
🍎

iPhone (NudgeIt app)

  1. Open the NudgeIt app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to Account Management.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Upon confirmation, all locally cached data is erased from your device immediately, an authenticated erasure request is sent to our backend, and your sign-in account is permanently invalidated across all devices.

Section 2

What Is Deleted

🗑️

Removed from our servers within 48 hours

  • Account profile — your email address, account tier, and usage counters (users/{uid}).
  • Paired watches — every watch linked to your account.
  • Phone pairing records — your device's entries under each paired watch, with pairing counts adjusted.
  • Push tokens — the device tokens used to deliver nudges.
  • Deduplication & rate-limit records scoped to your account.
  • Authentication account — your Google / Apple sign-in link to NudgeIt is permanently deleted.

Temporary nudge/alarm status records are automatically purged by a scheduled maintenance job. A secondary server-side process sweeps for any leftover records as a safety net within the 48-hour window.

Section 3

Before You Delete

⚠️

Please note

Irreversible: deletion is permanent — your data cannot be recovered afterward.

Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your account does not cancel an active Premium subscription. Cancel it to avoid future charges:

  • Android: Google Play app → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → NudgeIt → Cancel.
  • iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → NudgeIt → Cancel.
Section 4

Can't Access the App?

If you're unable to open the app, you can still request deletion by email. Send a message to nudgeupservice@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address associated with your account, or submit a request via the support page under Account Management & Data Deletion Requests. Email requests are processed within 30 days.
