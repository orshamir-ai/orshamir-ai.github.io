How it works

Three steps to your first nudge

1

Install both apps

Install NudgeIt on your iPhone. Install NudgeIt Watch on any Apple Watch — it doesn't have to be the one paired to your phone.

2

Pair in seconds

Tap "Pair Phone" on the watch to get a 6-digit code. Enter it in the iPhone app. Done — the devices are linked.

3

Nudge

Tap the NUDGE button. The watch vibrates continuously until the wearer presses and holds Stop. You see confirmation instantly.
