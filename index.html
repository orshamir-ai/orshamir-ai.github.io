How it works

Built around two deliberate design decisions.

Hold-to-Stop Gesture

The 2-Second Ring Gesture

Stopping a nudge requires pressing and holding the watch screen for a full 2 seconds while an animated progress ring fills. This is not an accident — it's physics in service of intent.

A light tap or accidental wrist movement cannot cancel the alarm. The wearer must consciously apply pressure and hold, which is only possible when they are awake, aware, and in control of their hand. The sender sees the confirmation the moment the hold completes.

Confirmation Loop

Real-Time Confirmation Loop

The moment the watch receives the nudge it writes a status back to the server. The iPhone reads this in real time — first showing the watch is vibrating, then confirming the exact moment the wearer stopped it.

This closes the loop for the sender: you don't wonder whether they woke up. You know — down to the second — when they acknowledged the signal. If the watch doesn't respond within 55 seconds, the app marks it as unreachable and lets you try again.
