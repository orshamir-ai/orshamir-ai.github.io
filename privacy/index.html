Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

NudgeIt is a personal project developed and maintained by Or Shamir, an independent developer. This policy describes exactly what data is collected, why it is collected, and how it is handled. The short version: only the data strictly necessary to operate the app is collected — nothing more.

Section 1

Who We Are

NudgeIt ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by Or Shamir, an independent developer. You can reach us at nudgeupservice@gmail.com.

Section 2

Authentication

🔐

Google Sign-In & Sign in with Apple

NudgeIt offers two third-party sign-in methods: Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple. When you authenticate through either provider, NudgeIt receives only your email address — no password, no contact list, no profile photo unless explicitly shared by you.

This email address is stored securely in Google Firebase Authentication and in the app's Firestore database, where it is used solely to identify your account. It is not used for marketing, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to any third party outside the Firebase infrastructure.

Sign in with Apple users may choose to hide their real email using Apple's private relay feature. NudgeIt accepts relayed addresses and does not require a real email to be disclosed.

Section 3

Push Notification Infrastructure

📡

FCM & APNS Device Tokens

NudgeIt's core function — delivering a haptic wake-up signal to an Apple Watch — requires the collection and storage of device push tokens. Specifically:

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token: generated by the Apple Watch device and uploaded to NudgeIt's Firestore database. This token is the address used to route haptic signaling commands from the sender's iPhone to the correct watch. Without it, push delivery is not possible.

Apple Push Notification service (APNs) token: the underlying Apple token that FCM bridges. This is handled internally by the Firebase SDK and is not stored separately by NudgeIt.

Tokens are associated with a randomly generated device identifier (not your name or email). They are refreshed automatically when they change, and deleted from our database when you unpair the watch or uninstall the app. Tokens are never shared with third parties and are never used for any purpose other than delivering NudgeIt haptic signals.

Section 4

Data Protection

🛡️

What NudgeIt Does Not Log

No physical location data is collected at any point. NudgeIt does not access GPS, coarse location, Wi-Fi network names, or any other location signal. The app does not know or store where you are.

No message contents are logged. NudgeIt signals carry no text, voice, image, or any user-composed content. The only data transmitted in a nudge payload is: a wake-up command identifier, a vibration profile name (gentle / normal / urgent), and the sender's display name as set by the sender. No private communication takes place through NudgeIt.

Alarm status is processed strictly to verify wake-up state. When a nudge is sent, a record is written to the database with a status field (pending → received → stopped). This record exists only to close the real-time confirmation loop — letting the sender know when the watch vibrated and when the wearer pressed Stop. It is not used for analytics, profiling, or any secondary purpose.

The following data types are never collected:

✕ Physical location
✕ Contacts / address book
✕ Microphone
✕ Camera
✕ Health data
✕ Message content
✕ Advertising ID
✕ Browsing history
Section 5

Full Data Inventory

  • Email address — for account sign-in and identification. Stored in Firebase.
  • iPhone device ID — a randomly generated identifier stored in the device Keychain and in Firebase, used to link your iPhone to paired watches.
  • Apple Watch device ID — a randomly generated identifier stored on the watch and in Firebase, used as the routing key for push delivery.
  • FCM push token — required to deliver nudge signals to the Apple Watch. Stored in Firebase, deleted on unpair.
  • Subscription tier — FREE or PREMIUM. Stored in Firebase to control feature access.
  • Alarm status records — pending / received / stopped. Stored temporarily in Firebase to power the real-time confirmation loop.
  • Activity history — a local log of nudge events stored only on your iPhone. It does not leave your device.

In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. NudgeIt never sees your payment details. We receive only the transaction result (success or failure) and store your subscription tier (FREE or PREMIUM) in our database.

Section 8

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase servers located in the United States. Firebase applies industry-standard encryption for data in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your own account through Firebase Security Rules — no other user or application can read it.

Section 9

Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. Push notification tokens are deleted when you unpair a watch or uninstall the app. Upon account deletion — whether initiated in-app or via an email request — all associated server-side data is permanently and irreversibly removed from our Firebase database within 48 hours. Locally cached data on your device is erased at the moment you confirm deletion in the app.

Section 10

Account Deletion

🗑️

In-App Self-Service Deletion

NudgeIt provides a native, self-service account deletion flow directly inside the iPhone app. No email request or waiting period is required to initiate deletion.

How to delete your account in the app:

  1. Open the NudgeIt iPhone app.
  2. Tap the Settings tab (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to the Account Management section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Read and confirm the deletion prompt.

Upon confirmation, the following actions occur immediately and automatically:

  • All locally cached data is erased from your device (watch list, activity history, cooldown state, preferences).
  • A secure server-side erasure request is dispatched to our Firebase backend via an authenticated API call.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account is permanently invalidated, ending all active sessions across devices.
🛡️

What Is Erased From Our Servers

The server-side erasure pipeline removes the following records from Google Firebase within 48 hours of the deletion request:

  • User profile — email address, account tier, creation timestamp, and daily usage counters stored in users/{uid}.
  • Paired watch list — all watch entries under users/{uid}/watches/*.
  • Phone pairing records — all entries in watches/{watchId}/paired_phones/ that are linked to your account, with the corresponding device pairing count decremented atomically.
  • Idempotency records — request deduplication tokens scoped to your user ID (request_ids collection).
  • Rate-limit counters — brute-force protection records in the pairing_attempts collection.
  • Firebase Authentication account — permanently deleted, invalidating all sign-in credentials.

Ephemeral alarm status records (alarms collection) generated during active nudge sessions are automatically purged by our scheduled maintenance job within 24 hours of expiry, independently of account deletion.

A secondary server-side process monitors for any records not caught by the primary deletion call and removes them as a safety net within the 48-hour window.

⚠️

Important Notes Before Deleting

Irreversible: Account deletion is permanent. Your data cannot be recovered after confirmation.

Subscription: Deleting your NudgeIt account does not automatically cancel an active Premium subscription. Cancel your subscription first via iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → NudgeIt to avoid future charges.

Alternative — email request: If you are unable to access the app, you may submit a deletion request by emailing nudgeupservice@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", or by submitting the form at orshamir-ai.github.io/support under the Account Management & Data Deletion Requests category. Manual requests are processed within 30 days.
