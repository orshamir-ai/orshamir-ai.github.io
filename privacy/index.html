Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

NudgeIt is built by an independent developer, Or Shamir. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and how it is handled. Your privacy is taken seriously — only the minimum data required to operate the app is collected.

1. Who We Are

NudgeIt ("the app", "we", "us") is developed and maintained by Or Shamir, an independent developer. You can contact us at nudgeupservice@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Account information
When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address. This is stored in our database to identify your account. We do not receive your password.

Device identifier (iPhone app)
A randomly generated device ID ("phone ID") is created when you first install the app. This ID is stored on your device (in the device Keychain) and in our database to link your iPhone to any Apple Watches you pair. It does not contain your name or any personally identifiable information.

Device identifier (Apple Watch app)
A randomly generated watch ID is created on the Apple Watch. This ID is used to receive push notifications and to link the watch to paired iPhones. It is stored in our database alongside the watch's push notification token.

Push notification token
The Apple Watch receives nudges via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Your watch's FCM token is stored in our database and is used solely to deliver nudge signals from the paired iPhone. It is updated automatically when it changes and deleted when you unpair.

In-app purchase information
If you subscribe to NudgeIt Premium, Apple processes the payment. We receive only the result of the transaction (success or failure) and store your subscription tier ("FREE" or "PREMIUM") in our database. We never see your payment details.

Usage data (activity history)
A local log of nudge events (sent, vibrating, woke up, timeout) is stored on your iPhone. This data does not leave your device except as described above.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Microphone or camera
  • Health or fitness data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history or usage analytics

4. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to operate the app:

  • Your email address identifies your account and enables sign-in.
  • Your device IDs link your iPhone and Apple Watch so nudge signals can be routed correctly.
  • Your push notification token is used to deliver nudge signals to your Apple Watch.
  • Your subscription tier controls which features are available in the app.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

5. Third-Party Services

NudgeIt uses the following third-party services to operate:

Google Firebase (Google LLC)
We use Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (data storage), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). Firebase is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.)
If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple handles authentication. Apple's Privacy Policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy

Sign in with Google (Google LLC)
If you choose to sign in with Google, Google handles authentication. Google's Privacy Policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

No other third parties have access to your data.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase servers, which are located in the United States. Firebase uses industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. We follow Firebase's recommended security rules to restrict access to your data to your own account only.

7. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data (email address, device IDs, paired watches, and nudge records) is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Push notification tokens are deleted when you unpair a watch or uninstall the app.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at nudgeupservice@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

NudgeIt is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
