Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Apple Watch need to be paired to the sender's iPhone?
No. Any Apple Watch running the NudgeIt Watch app can be paired — it does not need to be the watch already associated with the sender's iPhone through Apple's system. The two devices communicate through the internet via NudgeIt's servers, not via Bluetooth or WatchConnectivity.
How do I pair my iPhone with an Apple Watch?

On the Apple Watch: open the NudgeIt Watch app and tap "Pair Phone". A 6-digit code appears on screen — it is valid for 5 minutes.

On the iPhone: tap "Pair New Watch", enter the 6-digit code and a name for the watch, then tap "Ready to Pair". The watch is linked immediately.

The watch is not vibrating when I tap NUDGE. What should I do?

1. Make sure the NudgeIt Watch app has been opened at least once after installation — it registers its push token on first launch.

2. Verify the watch has an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular).

3. If the iPhone shows "Open the watch app, then try again", open NudgeIt on the watch — it will refresh its connection automatically.

4. If the issue persists, unpair and re-pair the watch from the iPhone app.

How do I stop the vibration on the Apple Watch?
Press and hold the large Stop button on the watch screen for 2 seconds while the progress ring fills. A deliberate hold is required — an accidental tap or wrist movement will not cancel the alarm. The sender's iPhone confirms the moment you stop it.
How many nudges can I send per day?
Free users can send 3 nudges per day using the Normal vibration profile. Premium users have unlimited nudges and access to all three profiles (Gentle, Normal, Urgent). The daily count resets at midnight.
How many watches can I pair?
Free users can pair 1 watch. Premium users can pair up to 3 watches.
How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → NudgeIt → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.
How do I delete my account?
Email us at nudgeupservice@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". We will permanently delete all data associated with your account within 30 days.
