Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Apple Watch need to be paired to the sender's iPhone?
No. Any Apple Watch running the NudgeIt Watch app can be paired — it does not need to be the watch associated with the sender's iPhone via Apple's system. The two devices communicate through the internet via NudgeIt's servers.
How do I pair my iPhone with an Apple Watch?
On the Apple Watch: open the NudgeIt Watch app and tap "Pair Phone" to generate a 6-digit code.

On the iPhone: tap "Pair New Watch", enter the 6-digit code along with a name for the watch, then tap "Ready to Pair". The pairing code is valid for 5 minutes.
The watch is not vibrating when I tap NUDGE. What should I do?
1. Make sure the NudgeIt Watch app is installed and open on the Apple Watch at least once after install.
2. Check that the watch has an internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular).
3. On the watch, open NudgeIt and check that reception is enabled.
4. If the issue persists, try unpairing and re-pairing the watch from the iPhone app.
The iPhone app shows "Open the watch app, then try again." What does this mean?
The watch's push notification token has expired or changed. Open the NudgeIt Watch app on the Apple Watch — it will refresh its connection automatically. Then try sending the nudge again from the iPhone.
How many watches can I pair?
Free users can pair 1 watch. Premium users can pair up to 3 watches.
How many nudges can I send per day?
Free users can send 3 nudges per day. Premium users have unlimited nudges. The daily count resets at midnight.
How do I stop the vibration on the Apple Watch?
Press and hold the large Stop button on the watch screen. A brief hold is required to confirm you're awake — a light tap won't stop it.
Can I use NudgeIt without an internet connection?
No. Both the iPhone and the Apple Watch need an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular) to send and receive nudges. The nudge signal travels through NudgeIt's servers.
How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → NudgeIt → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.
How do I delete my account?
Email us at nudgeupservice@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and we will permanently delete all data associated with your account within 30 days.
